Security

on the screen“stop STOP”is software, not an emergency stop.
  • Before wiring, repairing, or touching the electrode: usephysical switchTurn off the high voltage power supply and 24 V drive power supply, wait for the voltage to return to zero.
  • screen display OFF, “Stopped”,does not mean that the electrode is out of power,There is no guarantee that the pump will stop. Go and see it on site.
  • The pump does not stop, the pipeline falls off, and the liquid overflows: first press the button on the side of the pump. 24 V power-off switch,Come back and look at the screen.
  • When discharging and turning the pump, people must be present, and the physical switch must be at hand.
  • When executing this program, do not open an independent laboratory at the same time v7 or Arduino IDE Serial port window.

First time use: do this checklist first

This version of the program and the new firmware for the pump control boardhas not been run on real devices yet (was only tested on simulated devices). So for the first time, please do it in the order below, and check each item only after you have really seen it.

rule: Stop if one thing is wrong. Don’t go down, don’t increase the voltage. Write down the message on the screen (also visible on the Alerts page).

1. Only look at (high voltage load and 24 V The drive power supply is disconnected)

2. Pump (only receives water; turn off the high pressure; put your hand on 24 V Next to the power-off switch)

three, discharge (30 V; The high voltage load is still disconnected and the wire ends are fixed)

4. Connect the load

The details of each item and ”what should be seen” are inDetailed steps 5.
