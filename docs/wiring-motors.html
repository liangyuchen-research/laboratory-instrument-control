0. Architecture: Two Identical Groups

Build and test one complete group before reproducing the same topology for the second group.
Each group contains one driver, one ULN2803A, four relays, and four motors, with 55 connections. Only the assigned pins differ between groups. Complete and test Group A first, then build Group B.

Separate ULN2803 devices make each motor group independently testable. A group can be disconnected for diagnosis without rewiring the other. Motor selection uses four channels on each IC, leaving four additional channels per IC before auxiliary devices are connected. In the complete v6 installation, ULN #1 channels 5–6 control the valves.

Initial Flow Estimate for RZ1030B-8 with 3 mm Tubing

The source notes cite an eight-roller pump with 3.0 mm ID × 1.0 mm wall tubing delivering approximately 114 mL/min at 400 rpm. This gives approximately 0.285 mL/revolution and an initial conversion of 5614.035 pulses/mL. This is a starting estimate, not a calibration certificate. Tubing material, wall thickness, compression, backpressure, and wear affect delivery.

Volume and mass targets use +10/+5/+1/−10/−5/−1 increments in the console. The configured speed conversion for motors 2–9 is 4237.008 pulses/mL. Volume dosing uses 40 mL/min, or 2825 steps/s. A recorded motor-2 check delivered 46.469 mL for a 50 mL command; the common dose-step coefficient was adjusted to 4558.961 pulses/mL. A 50 mL command therefore uses 227948 steps at 2825 steps/s, approximately 80.690 s, for every motor. These shared settings do not establish equal physical delivery across pumps. Mass-feedback dosing uses 40→10→3 mL/min, corresponding to 2825→706→212 steps/s, and stops within the configured ±0.5% target band. Prime the tubing before use. Mass dosing controls grams directly and does not require liquid density.

2. ULN2803A Channel Mapping

Connect both pin 9 and pin 10 on each IC. Pin 9 provides the current return; pin 10 connects the internal clamp diodes to +24V. Missing clamp connections can expose the IC to relay turn-off transients.
Inputs use pins 1–4 and outputs use pins 18/17/16/15: input/output pairs face diagonally across the package. Ground only genuinely unused inputs. In v6, ULN #1 inputs 5–6 are assigned to the solenoid valve relays.

Star wiring: driver #1, driver #2, Group A control, and Group B control should use four separate branches from the supply terminals. Do not supply one driver through the other driver's terminals. Shared wire impedance can couple motor-current transients into the other group.

7. Commands and Testing

CommandEffect
S,<n>Select motor n (2–9), using the group's interlocked relay-switching sequence
V,<n>,<speed>Continuous motion; negative speed reverses direction. Motors 2/9 open their valve 1 s before motion; speed 0 stops the motor and closes its valve after 1 s.
P,<n>,<steps>,<speed>Move a fixed number of steps and stop. Motors 2/9 automatically include valve lead and trailing delays.
CCleaning: fill for 30 s, overlap for 45 s, then drain for 45 s. Water runs at 0–75 s; OUT runs at 30–120 s, measured from water-motor start.
XStop All: stop/release both groups, deselect relays, stop the syringe pump and scale stream, and close valves.
?Returns STATE,a=<motor>,sa=<state>,b=<motor>,sb=<state>
Concurrent operation example:V,3,400 starts Group A motor 3; then V,7,-400 starts Group B motor 7 in reverse. Sending V,4,400 switches Group A from motor 3 to motor 4 while Group B motor 7 continues running.
The firmware switching sequence is: release the group driver → open all group relays → select the target relay → enable the driver. It affects only the selected group and is enforced by the firmware.

8. Checklist

  • Both ULN pin-10 connections go to +24V.
  • Both ULN pin-9 connections share Mega GND and 24V negative.
  • ULN #1 IN1–4 map to D2–D5; ULN #2 IN1–4 map to D6–D9.
  • Outputs map in order: pin 18→first relay, 17→second, 16→third, 15→fourth.
  • Both drivers' PUL−/DIR−/ENA− terminals connect to Mega GND (six connections).
  • COM jumpers do not cross between groups(2–5 in one chain, 6–9 in the other).
  • NC terminals 1–4 are unconnected; NO terminals map 5→A+, 6→A−, 7→B+, 8→B−.
  • Each motor's coil pairs have been checked with a multimeter.
  • Both DM542J DIP banks are ON/OFF/ON/OFF/OFF/OFF/ON/ON: eight microsteps, 1600 pulses/revolution.
  • The 24V supply uses four star-wired motor/control branches; the source design suggests ≥8A.
  • All 111 documented motor-system leads are labeled.

9. Troubleshooting

SymptomChecks
No response from an entire groupCheck the group's ULN pins 9/10, driver supply, and control signals.
One motor cannot be selectedTrace Dn to its group input/output channel and relay. Check the corresponding relay LED first.
Selecting Group B stops Group ACheck for a cross-group COM jumper or joined driver outputs. Disconnect power before inspection.
The previous relay does not release during a selection changeCheck relay-coil wiring, ULN output mapping, and shorts between control pins.
Motor 5 or 9 has reduced torqueCheck accumulated voltage drop across jumper crimps. If necessary, use a dedicated driver-output branch to the final relay while preserving group isolation.
Missed steps or resets with both groups activeCheck star wiring and power-supply capacity.
A ULN device becomes hotDisconnect power immediately. Check IC orientation and whether pin 10 was connected to supply negative.
