1. Overview

The scale uses a separate serial interface from the motors and valves: Mega Serial2(D16/D17). Motors use D2–D9 and D22–D27; valves use D10/D11. The syringe pump occupies Serial1(D18 / D19), so the scale uses Serial2. The scale adapter's RS485 direction-control pin is D36. D26, used by an earlier standalone scale setup, is already assigned to driver 2 DIR in this system.

The chain uses two supply levels: 24V for the transmitter and 5V for the specified adapter. The load cell receives excitation from the transmitter's E+/E− terminals. Never connect 12V or 24V directly to E+/E−.

3. The Four Load-Cell Leads

The load cell contains a four-strain-gauge Wheatstone bridge. E+/E− carry excitation from the transmitter, while S+/S− carry the bridge's differential output of only a few millivolts.

Lead colorTransmitter terminalFunction
RedE+Excitation positive (EXC+)
BlackE−Excitation negative (EXC−)
GreenS+Signal positive (SIG+)
WhiteS−Signal negative (SIG−)
Reversed Bridge Polarity Reversing E+/E− can invert the signal or produce an unexpected zero reading. Reversing S+/S− makes the reading move negative when a mass is added. Check the identified pair and correct its polarity with power off before repeating the test.

4. Resistance Checks Before Power-Up

Wire colors vary by load-cell manufacturer. Confirm the datasheet and use resistance checks on the disconnected load cell as supporting evidence:

Resistance patternInterpretation
Highest-resistance pair (for example, 1000 Ω)may identify E+ and E−(input resistance)
Another pair near the rated resistance (1000 Ω or slightly lower in the source example)may identify S+ and S−(output resistance)
An excitation-to-signal measurement near 3/4is consistent with a bridge-arm path rather than a short.

Resistance alone may not distinguish equal bridge pairs; verify the load-cell specification. If loading produces a negative reading after correct pair identification,reverse S+ and S− with power off, then test again.

5. Transmitter Power (⑤⑥)

#FromTo
⑤24V supply +VTransmitter V+ / PWR+ / 12~24V
⑥24V supply −V(GND)Transmitter V− / PWR− / GND
Do not connect these power leads to E+/E−. E+/E− are load-cell excitation outputs, not power inputs. Applying supply voltage there can damage the analog front end. Use the labeled power-input terminals and check the indicator after power-up.

6. RS485 Pair (⑦⑧) and Optional Signal Ground (⑨)

#From transmitterTo adapter RS485 side
⑦A / 485+ / D+A(sometimes labeled A+)
⑧B / 485− / D−B(sometimes labeled B−)
⑨(optional)GND / SGAdapter GND

Lead ⑨ is redundant in the documented short-cable topology because transmitter ground reaches 24V negative through ⑥, and connection ⑮ joins that node to Mega and adapter ground. Longer runs above 2–3 m or noisy installations require a considered signal-ground and shielding arrangement.

RS485 A/B labels differ between manufacturers. If communication fails after checking the identified signal leads,power down and reverse A and B, then test again. Do not interchange power and signal connections.

9. Firmware and Commands

Scale support is integrated into firmware/Arduino/Arduino.ino and requires no additional Arduino. The startup message is READY,arduino+pump+2valves+scale.

#define SCALE_SERIAL  Serial2     // D16 = TX2, D17 = RX2
#define SCALE_DE_PIN  36          // Joined DE/RE connection
CommandEffect
W,READRead once (the console normally uses the 10 Hz stream) → WEIGHT,<grams>,RAW,<raw>,ST,<1=stable/0=changing>
W,STREAM,<Hz>Enable continuous reporting; 10 Hz at startup(W,STREAM,0 disables it)
W,TARETare with the empty container in place.
W,CAL,100Calibrate the scale after placing a 100 g reference mass.
W,SCANScan baud rates, addresses, and registers; useful when the transmitter does not respond
W,SET,1,3,0,2Apply scan results: address, function code, register, and format.
W,SNIFF / W,LOOPListen-only and adapter diagnostic modes for investigating communication faults.
W,CFGPrint the current settings.
Stepper service continues during scale reads. A Modbus read may wait up to 250 ms. The firmware continues calling serviceMotors() inside the wait loop to service motor pulses. Physical timing and missed-step behavior still require hardware validation.
Continuous acquisition starts at 10 Hz. The firmware default for wStreamHz is 10. After three consecutive read failures, streaming stops and reports ERR,stream_stopped_no_reply. The console displays a warning. Correct the wiring, then select Restart Acquisition.
W,SCAN, W,SET The console has no buttons for these low-level settings, but the backend exposes scale.diagnose and scale.modbus through POST /fn/…. Alternatively, stop the backend and use the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor; only one process can open the port at a time.

10. Initial Calibration

StepActionExpected result
1With power off, connect load-cell leads ①–④ and power leads ⑤⑥; verify terminals before applying power.The transmitter power indicator lights.
2With power off, complete ⑦⑧ and ⑩–⑮, then connect Mega USB.—
3Run run.bat to open the console.The scale starts streaming at 10 Hz; initial readings are not yet calibrated.
4If no data arrives, call POST /fn/scale.diagnose {"mode":"scan"} or stop the backend and send W,SCANthrough the Serial Monitor. Record the baud rate, address, and register; use W,SET to apply them.
5With an empty container, select Tare Empty ContainerThe reading approaches zero.
6Place the reference mass, select its value, then select Calibrate with Reference MassThe reading matches the reference mass within the measurement uncertainty.
7Remove the reference mass.The reading returns near zero.
Calibration settings are saved. Console calibration overwrites config/scale_calibration.json and loads it at the next startup or reconnection. The software restores calibration after a reset; verify tare and accuracy whenever the mechanical setup or reference conditions change.
Response to Small Mass Changes: The current configuration is W,AVG,1, so live readings use no moving average and display three decimal places. This improves responsiveness but exposes vibration and electrical noise. Display precision is not measurement accuracy.

Connection Checklist

  • Load cell: red→E+, black→E−, green→S+, white→S−, verified for the installed cell.
  • 24V connects to V+/V−,not E+ / E−
  • Transmitter A→adapter A and B→B, with polarity checked.
  • Adapter VCC→rated supply voltage and GND→GND.
  • RO→D17, DI→D16(correct direction)
  • Joined DE/RE connects to D36(unconnected for an automatic-direction adapter).
  • 24V negative and Mega GND share the documented common-ground link.
  • Upload firmware/Arduino/Arduino.ino and verify that the startup message contains +scale
  • Tare and reference-mass calibration have been completed.
