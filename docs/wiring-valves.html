1. Overview

Why use a relay instead of driving the valve directly from the ULN2803?
The system includes a load cell. Driving the approximately 0.4A valve through the ULN2803 would route its current through the shared control ground, increasing measurement disturbances.
Relay contacts provide a separate path: +24V → contact → valve → 24V−. The valve current does not flow through the control-ground branch.

4. Electrical Checks Before Power-Up

4.1 Measure Coil Resistance and Estimate Power

For a resistive coil at 24V, estimate its steady-state power and current from the measured resistance:

Power (W) = 576 ÷ resistance (Ω) Current (A) = 24 ÷ resistance (Ω)
Measured resistancePowerCurrent
Approximately 29 Ω20 W0.83 A
Approximately 58 Ω10 W0.42 A
Approximately 72 Ω8 W0.33 A
Approximately 115 Ω5 W0.21 A

The documented examples are in the tens-of-ohms range. Confirm the installed coil specification and relay DC inductive-load rating; the listed 10A contact rating alone does not define switching endurance for an inductive load.

4.2 Measure Both Polarities to Check for a Diode

ResultInterpretation
The two readings are similarThe coil may have no internal diode. Retain the documented red-positive polarity.
The readings are substantially differentA diode or LED may be present: red must remain positive. Reversed polarity can produce excessive current.
If a diode is present, use the higher resistance reading as an approximate coil-resistance estimate when the diode is reverse-biased.

8. Installing the 1N4007 Diodes

Fit one diode across the red and black leads of each valve.
Connect the banded cathode to red and the anode to black.
Without suppression, the coil's turn-off transient can arc across relay contacts and couple interference into RS485 and load-cell wiring, disturbing scale readings.
The ULN2803 clamp diodes suppress the relay coils when its COM connection is correctly wired.
Mechanical installation: Use appropriate crimp or terminal-block connections to join red with the diode cathode and black with the anode. Insulate exposed leads and prevent shorts.

?The report contains valves=10: the first bit is valve 1, the second is valve 2; 1 means open.
Manual valve control is not exposed. The console has no open/close/pulse buttons, and the HTTP API has no valves.* functions. The valves follow motors 2 and 9 to maintain the required timing and route selection.
Valve current bypasses Arduino ground wiring. The path is +24V → relay contact → valve → 24V−. Separate power branches and correctly oriented flyback diodes reduce interference with weighing and stepper control.

11. Checklist and Troubleshooting

  • Both valve coil resistances and power estimates have been recorded.
  • Both relay coil polarities are confirmed: pin 13 positive, LED operating.
  • ULN #1 OUT5 and OUT6 are pins 14 and 13 respectively, distinct from relay terminal numbers.
  • Relay pins 1, 4, 8, and 12 are unconnected; feedback is not implemented.
  • Each valve has a 1N4007 across its red and black leads,with the banded end connected to red
  • . NC pins 1 and 4 remain unconnected.
  • Valve power comes directly from the supply, not the Arduino or ULN branch.
  • Prime the fluid path before operating the valves, as specified for the installed hardware.
SymptomChecks
Relay does not operateCheck the ULN output connection: OUT5 is pin 14, not pin 5. Check relay coil polarity and LED indication.
