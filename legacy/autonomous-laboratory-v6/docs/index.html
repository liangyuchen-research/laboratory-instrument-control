Hardware wiring guides

1 · Relays and peristaltic pumps

8 stepper-driven pumps

Two DM542J drivers, eight MY4N-GS relays, and two ULN2803A arrays. Each group of four motors shares one driver through relay selection with the driver disabled during switching. Groups A and B can operate concurrently, with one selected motor per group.

D2–D9 relays · D22–D24 / D25–D27 drivers
2 · Solenoid valves

2 normally closed interlocked valves

24 VDC valves use ULN2803A-driven LB2HN-24DS relays. Motors 2 (water) and 9 (CuSO₄) control valves 1 and 2 with 1 s pre-open and post-stop delays. Cleaning runs water for 30 s, water and OUT together for 45 s, then OUT motor 8 alone for 45 s.

D10 / D11 → ULN#1 IN5 / IN6 · automatic only
3 · Syringe pump

RUNZE SY-01B

RS485 on Serial1. The guide covers mirrored solder-side and mating-side views of the female DB15 connector, identification of the three cable bundles, and conversion between microlitres and steps.

D18 = TX1 ‧ D19 = RX1 ‧ DB15 pin 1/9/10/11/12
4 · Weight module

Load cell and weighing transmitter

Four-wire load-cell identification, separate transmitter power and excitation terminals, Serial2 RS485 with a direction-control pin, and a three-stage diagnostic procedure for missing readings.

D17 = RX2 ‧ D16 = TX2 ‧ D36 = DE+RE

Shared setup requirements

A COM port can be opened by only one process. Close Arduino Serial Monitor while the backend runs. Before flashing firmware, press Ctrl+C in the service terminal to stop the backend.
Edit pin assignments in configuration. The wiring guides follow config/hardware.yaml. To change pins: edit YAML → run python tools/gen_firmware_config.py → reflash firmware → restart the backend. Duplicate assignments prevent startup and produce a message identifying both conflicts.
Use star wiring for the 24 V supply. Run separate supply branches to the motor drivers, valves, syringe pump, and weighing transmitter. Daisy-chained supply wiring can cause voltage drops when a load starts, leading to Arduino resets or disturbed weight readings during valve switching.
